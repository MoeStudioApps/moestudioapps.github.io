# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use **Goofy Bumpy: Arena Mayhem** ("the Game").

## 1. Information We Collect

Goofy Bumpy: Arena Mayhem does not require users to create an online account.

Game information such as player progress, unlocked fighters, coins, upgrades, settings, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on the user's device.

A player name or nickname entered inside the Game may also be stored locally for gameplay purposes.

We do not operate our own server for collecting or storing this information.

## 2. Advertising

The Game uses third-party advertising services, including **Google AdMob**.

These advertising services may process information necessary to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalized or non-personalized advertising depending on the user's region, settings, and consent.

Advertising providers operate according to their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Consent

Where required by applicable law, the Game may ask for consent before personalized advertising or certain advertising-related data processing takes place.

Users may be able to modify their consent or privacy choices through available privacy settings.

## 4. Rewarded Advertising

The Game may offer optional rewarded advertisements.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is voluntary. Players may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit such as additional coins or other rewards.

## 5. In-Game Data

Game progress may include:

- Coin balance
- Fighter ownership
- Fighter upgrades
- Power-up inventory
- Daily rewards
- Gameplay progress
- Player settings
- Player nickname

This information is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Removing the Game or clearing its application data may result in the loss of locally stored progress.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party services necessary for advertising, operating, or distributing the Game.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Game does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from players.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information has been provided improperly may contact us using the contact information below.

## 8. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information handled by the Game. However, no electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change.
